PIZZA DELIVERY ANALYTICS - DAX MEASURES
=============================================

HOW TO ENTER EACH ONE
---------------------
1. Home ribbon -> New measure
2. The formula bar opens showing:   Measure =
3. Select it all (Ctrl+A) and paste ONE measure from below
4. Press Enter (or click the tick on the left of the formula bar)
5. Repeat

Enter them TOP TO BOTTOM. Later ones reference earlier ones - for example
[Avg Order Value] needs [Revenue] and [Orders] to already exist. Out of order
and you get "cannot be determined" errors.

Multi-line measures (14, 16, 18, 20): paste the whole thing at once. Do NOT
press Enter between the lines - Enter commits the measure.

Do not paste any line starting with -- ; those are comments.


1
Revenue = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[gross_revenue])

2
Net Revenue = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[net_revenue])

3
Orders = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[orders])

4
Avg Order Value = DIVIDE([Revenue], [Orders])

5
Contribution Margin = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[contribution_margin])

6
Operating Profit = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[operating_profit])

7
Operating Margin % = DIVIDE([Operating Profit], [Net Revenue])

8
Food Cost % = DIVIDE(SUM('mart kpi_daily'[food_cost]), [Revenue])

9
Labour Cost % = DIVIDE(SUM('mart kpi_daily'[labour_cost]), [Revenue])

10
Fixed Costs = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[fixed_cost])

11
Franchise Fees = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[royalty_cost])

12
Commission Paid = SUM('mart kpi_daily'[commission_total])

13
Revenue Last Week = CALCULATE([Revenue], DATEADD('mart dim_date'[full_date], -7, DAY))

14
Revenue WoW % = DIVIDE([Revenue] - [Revenue Last Week], [Revenue Last Week])

15
Revenue YTD = TOTALYTD([Revenue], 'mart dim_date'[full_date])

16
Revenue 7d Avg =
AVERAGEX(
    DATESINPERIOD('mart dim_date'[full_date], MAX('mart dim_date'[full_date]), -7, DAY),
    [Revenue]
)

17
Avg Delivery Minutes = AVERAGE('mart fct_order'[actual_min])

18
Late Rate =
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('mart fct_order'), 'mart fct_order'[is_late] = TRUE()),
    CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('mart fct_order'), 'mart fct_order'[is_delivery] = TRUE())
)

19
Margin per Order =
DIVIDE(SUM('mart fct_order'[gross_margin]), COUNTROWS('mart fct_order'))

20
Aggregator Share % =
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE([Revenue], 'mart dim_channel'[is_aggregator] = TRUE()),
    [Revenue]
)


AFTER ALL 20 - FORMAT THEM
--------------------------
Click each measure in the Data pane, then use the Measure tools ribbon:

  Currency (Revenue, Net Revenue, Operating Profit, Contribution Margin,
            Avg Order Value, Fixed Costs, Franchise Fees, Commission Paid,
            Margin per Order, Revenue Last Week, Revenue YTD, Revenue 7d Avg)
      -> Format: Currency, Symbol EUR, 0 decimals (2 for the per-order ones)

  Percentage (Operating Margin %, Food Cost %, Labour Cost %, Revenue WoW %,
              Late Rate, Aggregator Share %)
      -> Format: Percentage, 1 decimal

  Whole number (Orders)
      -> Format: Whole number, with thousands separator

Unformatted measures show as 1084309.01 instead of EUR 1,084,309 - the single
biggest difference between a report that looks finished and one that does not.


SANITY CHECK
------------
Drop these on a blank page as cards. If the model is right you should see:

  Revenue            ~ EUR 1,103,270
  Net Revenue        ~ EUR 1,084,309
  Orders                    40,633
  Avg Order Value    ~ EUR 27.15
  Operating Profit   ~ EUR 148,950
  Operating Margin % ~ 13.7 %
  Commission Paid    ~ EUR 25,496

If Operating Profit shows blank, the measure order was wrong - recreate it
after [Operating Profit] and [Net Revenue] both exist.
